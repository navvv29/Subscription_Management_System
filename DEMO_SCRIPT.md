Subscript One — 3-Minute Demo Script

1. Introduction (0:00–0:25)

Hello everyone!

Today, we are presenting Subscript One, a Subscription Management and Operations Command Center.

Many businesses still manage recurring subscriptions, quotations, invoices and payments manually. This creates problems such as missed billing dates, duplicate invoices, calculation errors and difficulty tracking outstanding payments.

Subscript One aims to bring these operations together in one platform.

2. Product and Plan Configuration (0:25–0:50)

First, the admin configures a product with its name, type, sale price and cost price.

The admin can also create product variants with additional price adjustments.

Next, we configure a subscription plan with its billing frequency, minimum quantity and availability.

We can also configure applicable taxes and discounts.

3. Quotation and Subscription (0:50–1:15)

Now, the staff member creates a quotation for a customer.

The quotation contains the selected products, quantities, pricing and validity period.

Once the customer accepts the quotation, it can be converted into a subscription.

The subscription stores its customer details, billing period, payment terms and next billing date.

4. Invoice Generation (1:15–1:55)

Now comes the important part: recurring billing.

We use the Process Due Invoices action to generate invoices for subscriptions that are due.

Let's open the generated invoice.

We can inspect the customer details, invoice lines, taxes, discounts and final amount.

An important reliability check is duplicate prevention.

Let's run the billing process again.

The system should not generate another invoice for the same billing period.

5. Payment Recording (1:55–2:20)

Next, we record a payment against the invoice.

We enter the payment amount, method, date and optional note.

The invoice balance should update correctly.

A fully paid invoice should display its paid status, while a partially paid invoice should retain its outstanding balance.

6. Finance Dashboard (2:20–2:45)

Finally, we return to the dashboard.

Here, we can review:

- Active subscriptions.
- Collected revenue and payments.
- Outstanding invoices.
- Overdue invoices.

These figures should reconcile with the underlying invoice and payment records.

7. Closing (2:45–3:00)

Subscript One brings subscription management, recurring billing, invoicing and financial visibility into one unified platform.

Our focus is on reliability, accuracy and ease of operations.

Thank you!

Demo Fallback Plan

If email delivery is unavailable:

- Demonstrate the available email workflow without claiming successful delivery.
- Explain that verified email-provider configuration is required.

If scheduled billing is unavailable:

- Use the authorized Process Due Invoices action.
- Explain that production scheduling has not been verified.

If deployment is unavailable:

- Use available local or recorded screenshots.
- Clearly identify anything that has not been demonstrated live.

If a feature is incomplete:

- State that it is incomplete or unverified.
- Do not present planned functionality as working functionality.

Demo Integrity Rule

Only demonstrate features that have actually been implemented and tested.

Never claim successful billing, payment processing, email delivery or duplicate prevention without supporting evidence.
